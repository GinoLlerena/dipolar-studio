- **Source:** [D9](../03-redesign-plan.md#3-key-decisions)

## Decision

- BSD-3-Clause for software the owner owns (decided 2026-11-07); third-party datasets keep their own terms.
- An independent reimplementation informed by review of the legacy source (not clean-room).
