# ADR-0019: Self-describing scientific outputs (D19)

- **Status:** Accepted, 2026-11-07 (plan v0.6, Phase 1 baseline)
- **Source:** [D19](../04-ai-ready-architecture.md#d19--self-describing-scientific-outputs)

## Decision

- Results carry validity flags, the estimand and its conventions, assumptions, coded warnings and provenance links.
- Every later AI claim must cite the fields it depends on (Phase 3).

## Verified by

Result-schema tests (M1/M2).
