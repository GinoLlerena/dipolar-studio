# V0.1 Selection and access: the benchmark corpus for V1

**Status: revised after round 68 (2026-11-18).**

This is the first step of V0 in the approved Phase 2 proposal (`docs/phase2-proposal.md`). It
gives:

- the selection protocol;
- where each candidate's numbers are;
- whether its tables are complete;
- its conventions and its rights basis.

No value is transcribed or fitted here. That is V0.2 and V0.3.

## 2. The selection protocol

A dataset is **eligible for V1** when all of these hold:

1. **The molecule is in V1's scope:** rigid, with a supported point group (C1, Cs, C2v, D2h, D3h
   or D6h).
2. **The medium is in scope:** an achiral, uniaxial nematic or smectic A.
3. **The observables:** ¹H–¹H couplings, tabulated numerically **as the source's dipolar
   couplings D**.
   - These are **published effective D under the source's spectral assumptions**: the
     isotropic J values the spectral fit held fixed, and whatever it assumed about anisotropic J.
   - They are imported as printed: the same sign, no J subtracted again, no conversion to 2D.
   - Couplings to another nucleus are V4.
4. **Each value has a reported uncertainty.** Its exact meaning (the confidence level, and the
   covariance between values) is recorded when the source states it. Otherwise it is recorded as
   *unspecified*.
   - **The reported errors are kept as printed** (V0.2).
   - **How they are used** (as weights, independent, at their printed scale) is **preregistered
     in V0.3** as a stated error model.
   - **What follows from them:** residuals and agreement are descriptive, or explicitly
     conditional on that error model. A calibrated p-value or interval is claimed only with a
     justified error model or a separate calibration.
5. **The conditions are stated:** solvent, temperature, solute concentration, and which solutes
   share each sample tube.
   - A source that leaves some of this unresolved may be selected **as development data
     only**, with each unknown marked (loading, tube identity, co-solutes).
   - It cannot serve as a holdout, or for any claim that depends on the missing item.
6. **The source.** A primary source, or a copy the authors published. A secondary reproduction is
   used only as a contextual cross-check.
7. **Over-determination.** At least one condition has more coupling classes than order
   parameters (ν ≥ 1 with fixed geometry).
8. **Rights.** A rights basis is recorded for each channel: local validation, the repository,
   releases, packages, and reports.

**What each dataset's record must give** (its README, in V0.2):
- the label-to-pair map and the published frame;
- the authors' geometry and its source;
- their spectral assumptions: the LEQUOR inputs (J values, the anisotropic-J treatment);
- their order-parameter analysis: the SHAPE objective, its weights, and any vibrational or other
  corrections;
- the uncertainty definition, or "unspecified";
- the grouping (paper series, run, tube, co-solutes);
- the published comparison targets, and their form (a table or a figure).

## 3. The candidates

### Selected: benzene (Taggar, Campbell, Yethiraj & Burnell, *J. Phys. Chem. B* 110, 1363, 2006)

**The data** are in the Supporting Information, a plain-text file
(DOI 10.1021/jp052592j.s001).
- **Benzene: 15 conditions, 45 coupling values,** D12, D13 and D14 (ortho, meta, para), each with
  its uncertainty in the last digits.
  - **Run 1:** six single-solute samples (1.31–8.32 mol %) and `mix1`.
  - **Run 2:** five single-solute samples (0.55–4.969 mol %) and `mix2.1`, `mix2.2`, `mix2.3`.
- **The mixed samples' compositions** (mol % benzene/tfb/tcb/tbb, from the paper's table
  footnotes):
  - `mix1`: 3.65/2.70/4.29/2.77;
  - `mix2.1`: 2.129/0.000/1.984/0.000;
  - `mix2.2`: 1.748/1.669/0.885/0.510;
  - `mix2.3`: 2.846/0.000/1.060/0.000.
- **The medium:** ZLI-1132.
  - **The temperature:** a nominal setting of **300.9 K**, made reproducible by a reference
    sample (a peak separation in a benzene/"magic mixture" standard, about 4450 Hz, ±2 Hz,
    about ±0.05 K).
  - That reference is a calibration of the setting, not an absolute temperature.

**What the paper says about the values.**
- The couplings come from LEQUOR fits.
- **The J inputs.** The values "depend on the choice of J". They are effective D under the
  authors' isotropic J inputs, which the paper does not tabulate for benzene. These are LEQUOR
  provenance: they matter for reproducing the spectrum-to-D step, while SHAPE starts from the
  printed D. They are to be recovered in V0.2, or recorded as unknown.
- **Anisotropic J.** The paper discusses neglecting it for the fluorine couplings of tfb. It
  says nothing specific about benzene's H–H couplings, so for benzene the treatment is recorded
  as **not stated**.
- **The frame relation.** For D6h, S_xx = S_yy = −S_zz/2 (z normal to the ring). The paper's
  prose on p. 1364 appears to reverse this relation. The axial relation is used, and the
  discrepancy is noted.
- The uncertainty's confidence level and covariance are **unspecified**.

**Its published targets.**
- S_zz per condition (paper, Tables 1–2), from SHAPE with the geometry of Diehl & Niederberger,
  *J. Magn. Reson.* 9, 495 (1973). That geometry has not been obtained.
- The frame: z normal to the ring. A D6h tensor is axial, so its in-plane orientation is
  arbitrary. The paper's "x along a C–halogen bond" applies to the substituted benzenes.

**What it can and cannot show.**
- **A scale check, not a reproduction.** The first row's D12 and the published S_zz imply an
  ortho H–H distance of 2.478 Å, plausible for benzene. The worked numbers are in the dataset's
  README, which is excluded from source archives.
  - But SHAPE fits all the couplings, not D12 alone: |D12/S_zz| differs between the first row and
    `mix1` by more than rounding allows.
  - So **a numerical reproduction of the published S_zz needs the authors' geometry and
    objective**, recovered in V0.2. Until then the comparison is *pending*.
- **The coupling ratios are the independent content.**
  - A rigid regular hexagon gives D13/D12 = 0.192450 and D14/D12 = 0.125000.
  - The measured ratios of the first row are 0.824 % and 1.275 % higher (the values are in the
    dataset's README).
  - **Plausible causes:** vibrational and reorientation–vibration averaging, which a rigid model
    ignores; and the spectral J assumptions.
  - The V1 result will show how a rigid fixed-geometry model fares against these values. **What
    it will show is not assumed here.**
- **The other solutes in the file.**
  - 1,3,5-trichloro- and tribromobenzene each have one coupling (ν = 0): scale checks only. The
    0.85 mol % tcb row is marked "from spectral width" and has no uncertainty.
  - 1,3,5-trifluorobenzene has one H–H class (ν = 0); its fluorine couplings are V4.

### Selected: ortho-dichlorobenzene (Chandrakumar & Burnell, *Bull. Magn. Reson.* 15, 199–204, 1993)

**The data** (tables on pp. 201–202):
- **Table I** (55 wt % ZLI-1132/EBBA): **9 temperatures, 301–330 K**.
- **Table II** (70 wt % 5CB/EBBA): **13 temperatures, 288–316 K**.
- Each row has four couplings with ± uncertainties: **22 conditions, 88 coupling values**.
- **The solute loading** is 1–2 mol %; the exact loading of each sample is **unknown**.
- **Tube identity** (one tube per mixture, or several) is **unknown**.
- **Co-solutes.** Fig. 1's spectrum, in 70 wt % 5CB/EBBA at **300 K** (Table II's 300 K row),
  shows trichlorobenzene peaks. That sample contained trichlorobenzene. For the other rows, the
  co-solutes are **unknown**.
- **So ortho-dichlorobenzene is selected as development data with partial sample metadata**
  (protocol 5).

**What the paper says about the values.**
- They are LEQUOR fits of D_ij and two chemical shifts. The isotropic J_ij are fixed to Diehl &
  Khetrapal, *Mol. Phys.* (1968), the paper's ref. 14, and printed under each table (J12 = J34 =
  8.00, J13 = J24 = 1.50, J14 = 0.30, J23 = 7.40 Hz).
- So the values are effective D under those J. The uncertainty definition is **unspecified**.

**Numbering and frame** (Fig. 1).
- H1–H2–H3–H4 are consecutive around the ring, with H1 and H4 next to the chlorine-bearing
  carbons.
- The frame: **y is the in-plane C2 axis**, x is in-plane and parallel to H2–H3, and z is normal
  to the ring.
- The classes:
  - ortho 12 = 34;
  - ortho 23;
  - meta 13 = 24;
  - para 14.
- With four classes and two order parameters, **ν = 2 at rank 2**.
- **The frame must be transformed.** V1's C2v convention (`docs/phase2/v1.0-design.md`, D-4)
  puts z on the C2 axis, with x and y in the two mirror planes. The right-handed map
  (x_V1, y_V1, z_V1) = (z_paper, x_paper, y_paper) does this:
  - z_V1 is the paper's in-plane C2 axis y;
  - x_V1 is the ring normal, which lies in the perpendicular mirror;
  - y_V1 is the paper's x, in the molecular plane.

  The map is a cyclic permutation, so it is right-handed. It is frozen before any component is
  compared.

**Its published targets.**
- **What exists.** S_zz and S_xx − S_yy, from SHAPE with the geometry of ref. 14, only as
  plotted points.
  - In **Fig. 2(a)** the filled markers are the SHAPE results. The open triangles and squares
    are the model's recalculation, and the open circles are scaled predictions.
  - The round 69 reviewer counted **7 filled markers for Table I's 9 temperatures and 8 for
    Table II's 13**. So not every condition has a target in Fig. 2.
  - **Fig. 3** plots the same SHAPE results against the reduced temperature T_r = T/T_NI, with
    T_NI = 333 K (Table I) and 317 K (Table II).
- **What V0.3 does.**
  - Digitize the filled markers of both figures.
  - Cross-check each Fig. 2 marker against Fig. 3 through T_NI.
  - Freeze a **condition-by-condition target list**, with the digitization precision declared.
  - A condition whose marker is missing, or whose match is ambiguous, stays *pending*.

## 4. Grouping and holdouts

**The group manifest** (frozen with V0.2's README):
- **Each paper is one group.**
- **Taggar et al.:**
  - Run 1 reuses one tube per single-solute series, by adding solute;
  - Run 2 uses separate tubes;
  - the mixed samples link benzene to its co-solutes in the same tube.
- **Chandrakumar & Burnell:** two series, one per mixture.

**Exposure.** The values of both selected sources have been seen by the author agent and by the
round 68 reviewer ([`exposure-log.md`](exposure-log.md)).
- So **both are development data**, and their comparisons are labelled "no independent holdout".
- A future holdout is **optional**. The custodian (the owner) chooses and keeps it unseen until
  V0.3 has frozen the model and the criteria.

**Preregistration (V0.3) comes before any V1 experimental fit,** development data included.

stored as a dataset, and the paper is not redistributed.

  (45 values; a completeness check of 15 conditions × 3).

  values** with uncertainties.
  - **Source tokens are preserved:** at 311 K in Table II, the D12 = D34 uncertainty is printed

  - two independent AI transcriptions are compared mechanically and the owner spot-checks.

  These may need the owner's library access (decision 5). Without them, the published-value
  comparisons stay *pending*. The V1 fits with their own geometry still run, labelled.

  basis is established.
