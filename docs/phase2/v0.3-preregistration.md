# Phase 2 V0.3: the preregistration

**Frozen on 2026-11-19, before any V1 fit of experimental data.** This document and its
machine-readable twin, [`v0.3-preregistration.json`](v0.3-preregistration.json), fix how V1
will fit the two development datasets, what it compares the fits with, and what counts as
agreement. The JSON is authoritative for every number. It is validated against a closed schema
([`v0.3-preregistration.schema.json`](v0.3-preregistration.schema.json)), in which every frozen
number is a constant and every object forbids unknown keys.

`tests/test_v0_preregistration.py` checks four things:
- the JSON against the code it freezes;
- its frames and pair orbits, against a construction made independently from the geometry;
- its inputs and targets, by SHA-256;
- **no restricted value in any tracked blob.** This last check runs only where the local files
  exist (`tools/datasets/restricted_scan.py`, §11).

The label rules and the report's entry point are code (`tools/validation/prereg_labels.py` and
`tools/validation/prereg.py`), tested in `tests/test_prereg_labels.py`. The entry point binds
the roster to the hash-verified couplings, and checks the cross-field invariants the schema cannot
express. Rounds 73–74 reviewed this step
([`round73-v0.3-preregistration.md`](../validation/round73-v0.3-preregistration.md)).

No experimental value has been fitted by V1. **Both datasets are development data**: their
values were seen while selecting and transcribing them ([`exposure-log.md`](exposure-log.md)).
So every comparison is labelled **"development data, no independent holdout"**. Some choices
were made after that exposure, and §6 says which.

**The ortho-dichlorobenzene values and digitized targets, and the benzene paper's order
parameters, stay local** (© ISMAR and © ACS; V0.1 §5). This document refers to them only by
condition, count, precision and SHA-256.

## 1. The model

- **The fit** is `dipolar_core.fixed.fit_fixed`, version `fixed-linear.1` (V1.1, tag
  `v1.1-fixed-fit`). Its settings are frozen as they are in the code: the rank thresholds, the
  weak-direction ratio 1e-6, and the adequacy level α = 1e-3.
- **One fit per condition.** Each condition (a sample, at a temperature) is fitted on its own
  couplings. Nothing is pooled across conditions.
- **A rigid, fixed geometry** (§2), an achiral uniaxial medium, ¹H–¹H couplings, and the nuclide
  table `codata2018-iupac.1`. There are no vibrational corrections and no internal motion.
- **The couplings enter as printed:** the published effective D, with their sign, no J
  subtracted, and no factor of 2. The coupling files are frozen by SHA-256 in the JSON.

## 2. The declared geometry

The authors' geometries are not available yet. Benzene's comes from Diehl & Niederberger (1973),
ortho-dichlorobenzene's from Diehl & Khetrapal (1968), and both need library access. So V1 fits
with **one declared geometry**:

- **The source:** J. Gauss and J. F. Stanton, *J. Phys. Chem. A* **104**, 2865–2868 (2000), the
  equilibrium structure of benzene: r_e(CC) = 1.3914 Å and r_e(CH) = 1.0802 Å.
- **The shape:** a planar regular hexagon with radial C–H bonds. The ortho H–H distance is
  r_CC + r_CH = 2.4716 Å. The ring positions of the protons are in the JSON.
- **For ortho-dichlorobenzene** this is a **benzene-based idealization**. H1–H4 sit on carbons
  3–6, and chlorine enters no coupling. The real molecule's ring distortions are ignored. Their
  size, and their effect on S_xx − S_yy, the component most sensitive to them, are **not
  bounded** here.

## 3. The error model

- **The primary model:** independent Gaussian errors, with σ as printed and at its printed scale.
  No covariance is used, because neither source reports one.
- **Its status.** Both sources leave σ's meaning *unspecified*. Couplings from one spectral fit
  are probably correlated. Every χ², p-value and standard error is therefore **conditional on
  this model**, and is reported as such.
- **Paper-frame standard errors** (ortho-dichlorobenzene) use the full fitted covariance. They
  are not combined from the components' separate errors. The steps:
  1. `FixedResult.covariance_b` is in the orthonormal basis b, where b = ORTHO_SCALE·s5.
  2. Its (b₁, b₂) block is converted to (a, c) = (S_zz, S_xx − S_yy) with J = diag(1/√(3/2), √2):
     Σ_ac = J Σ_b J.
  3. It is converted to the paper's frame with the conversion matrix M of §5:
     Σ_paper = M Σ_ac Mᵀ.
- **A secondary, descriptive report:** standard errors scaled by √(χ²/ν) when ν > 0. This is a
  description of misfit, not a calibrated interval.
- **Adequacy** (the χ² test at α = 1e-3) is reported for every condition. It **does not enter the
  labels** (§7).

## 4. Benzene (Taggar et al. 2006)

- **Data:** `datasets/benzene-zli1132-taggar2006/couplings.csv`, which has 15 conditions × 3
  couplings.
- **The model:** D6h. The frame is V1's (D-4): z along C6, normal to the ring, and x along one
  C2′. There is one order parameter, S_zz. The pair orbits are D12 (H1–H2), D13 (H1–H3) and
  D14 (H1–H4). That gives 3 observations, d = 1 and ν = 2.
- **The estimand:** S_zz for each condition. The paper's frame also has z normal to the ring.
  V0.1 noted that the paper's prose on p. 1364 appears to reverse the axial relation; the relation
  used is S_xx = S_yy = −S_zz/2.
- **The roster:** 15 cells, one per condition.
- **The targets:** the paper's Tables 1–2, which give S_zz with its printed uncertainty, for all
  15 conditions.
  - The local file is `datasets-local/benzene-taggar2006-targets/targets.csv`, SHA-256
    `af653a83…efcfb`.
  - It is made by `tools/datasets/taggar2006_targets.py` from **two independent extraction
    implementations of one text layer**: pypdf's text flow, and pdfminer's positioned lines. They
    share the table identities, the token pattern and the key normalization. Their output is
    identical, token for token, and its keys are exactly the SI's 15 conditions.
  - Because they read one text layer, **the owner's spot-check against the rendered page is
    kept** (§10).
- **Flags:**
  - the four mixed samples share their tubes with co-solutes;
  - run 1's single-solute series shares one tube, so those conditions are not independent
    samples.

  Both are reported; neither excludes a condition.
- **Known before the fit (disclosed).** V0.1 found that the measured D13/D12 and D14/D12 are about
  1 % above the rigid hexagon's ratios. The printed σ are of order 10⁻⁵ relative. So the χ² test
  under the primary error model **is expected to reject the rigid model** for benzene. That is a
  statement about rigidity, about vibrational averaging and about the spectral assumptions. It is
  not about the implementation. It is reported, and it does not set the label.

## 5. ortho-Dichlorobenzene (Chandrakumar & Burnell 1993)

- **Data:** the local `datasets-local/odcb-chandrakumar1993/couplings.csv` (SHA-256 in the JSON).
  It has 22 conditions (Table I: 9 temperatures; Table II: 13) × 4 couplings.
- **The model:** C2v. The four pair orbits are D12 = D34, D13 = D24, D14 and D23 (one
  representative each), so N = 4, d = 2 and ν = 2.
- **The frame.** The fit runs in V1's C2v frame: z on the C2 axis, x and y in the mirror planes.
  - **The map:** (x, y, z)_V1 = (z, x, y)_paper. The JSON stores it as the matrix of V1 axes in
    paper coordinates.
  - **The conversion.** With a = S_zz and c = S_xx − S_yy in the V1 frame, the JSON's conversion
    matrix gives S_zz (paper) = (c − a)/2 and S_xx − S_yy (paper) = −(3a + c)/2.
  - **How the test checks it.** It builds the axes independently from the geometry: the ring
    normal, the C2 axis through the midpoint of H2–H3, and y = z × x. It builds the orbits from
    the symmetry operations. A synthetic tensor fitted through the JSON's map converts back
    through the JSON's matrix.
- **The estimands:** S_zz and S_xx − S_yy in the paper's frame, for each condition.
- **The roster:** 44 cells (22 conditions × 2 components).
- **The targets are digitized,** because the paper gives its order parameters only as plotted
  points.
  - **Fig. 3 is the primary source.** V0.1 planned to digitize both figures and cross-check them.
    Fig. 3 (against T_r = T/T_NI, with T_NI = 333 K for Table I and 317 K for Table II) plots
    filled markers only. Each panel has exactly 9 triangles and 13 squares, one per condition.
    Fig. 2 mixes filled with open symbols. So Fig. 3 gives the targets, and **Fig. 2 is the
    cross-check**.
  - **The method.** The tool is `tools/datasets/digitize_figure.py`. It decodes the embedded
    scan losslessly, fits each frame's four edges, and calibrates along the 14 ticks of each
    vertical side, so the scan's skew is corrected. It classifies markers by size and fill, and
    excludes the legend. Two overlapping pairs in S_zz (a stacked pair, and a side-by-side pair)
    are split using the known marker size; their 4 targets carry the flag `overlap-resolved`.
- **The assignment rule (frozen).** A marker is a condition's target only under three
  conditions:
  - its own T_r is within **0.0015** of T/T_NI; that is below half the closest spacing of two
    conditions, 0.0032;
  - it is the only such marker of its series;
  - it matches no other condition.

  Otherwise the cell is *pending*, flagged `no-marker` or `ambiguous`. Rank order is not used.
  **All 44 cells are assigned, and the largest |T_r − T/T_NI| is 0.0009.**
- **The Fig. 2 cross-check (frozen).** A Fig. 2 filled marker checks a cell when three
  conditions hold: it lies within ±1 K of that condition, it lies within 1 K of no other
  condition, and no other marker of its series lies within ±1 K of that condition. Each check is
  recorded as one of four outcomes:
  - **agrees**: within twice the summed precisions. That is 18 cells (17 S_xx − S_yy, 1 S_zz).
    The largest difference is 0.37 of the summed precisions.
  - **disagrees**: beyond that, which makes the cell pending. No cell does.
  - **unavailable**: no usable Fig. 2 marker. That is 24 cells: Fig. 2a's filled markers sit on
    the open "recalculated" symbols.
  - **ambiguous**: 2 cells, at Table II's 304 K and 305 K, which are 1 K apart.
- **The declared precision is a resolution estimate,** not a measured error:
  - half a pixel, plus the tick-calibration residual;
  - for a triangle, h/6: the offset between an ideal triangle's bounding-box centre (the point
    used) and its centroid;
  - for a split pair, one more pixel, a heuristic.

  | Component | Squares (Table II) | Triangles (Table I) |
  |---|---|---|
  | S_zz | 0.00027 to 0.00049 | 0.00087 to 0.00108 |
  | S_xx − S_yy | 0.00027 to 0.00028 | 0.00084 to 0.00092 |
- **The result:** 44 targets, 0 pending. The local file is
  `datasets-local/odcb-chandrakumar1993/targets.csv`, SHA-256 `b8e5c244…b8f50`.
- **Table II's 300 K and 302 K rows** are almost identical (V0.2). Both enter as printed, as two
  conditions, each against its own target. The sensitivity check is in §7.
- **Other flags:**
  - the 300 K Table II sample also contained trichlorobenzene;
  - the two colon tokens are read as decimals (V0.2);
  - the transcription is two identical AI transcriptions, and **the owner's spot-check is
    pending**. Until it is done, every comparison carries that note.

## 6. The agreement criteria

Two kinds of comparison are kept apart, as the V1.5 report requires.

**Independent support** compares V1's fit, made with the declared geometry, against the published
values. With p the target's precision (its printed σ for benzene, its digitization precision for
ortho-dichlorobenzene):
- S_zz agrees when |S − S_pub| ≤ 0.05·|S_pub| + 2p;
- S_xx − S_yy agrees when |S − S_pub| ≤ 0.05·|S_zz,pub| + 2p. This is an **axial-scale
  heuristic**: it stays sensible near S_xx − S_yy = 0, but allows a larger relative discrepancy in
  the anisotropy itself. A cell is compared only when that condition's published S_zz exists.

**What is shared, and what stays local.** For each cell, the local report shows the target, the
fit, the signed discrepancy and its margin, not only the verdict. That report stays in
`datasets-local/`: from the fit and a signed discrepancy, a reader could recover a restricted
target by subtraction.

What may be shared (the V1.5 page, an issue, a release) is `prereg.shareable`: per dataset, each
label with its counts, the drop-one labels, and the protocol revision. There are no per-cell
targets, discrepancies or margins. Fits may be shared only for cells whose targets are not
restricted. The benzene couplings, from the CC BY-NC SI, may be shared with their attribution.

Agreement here
means **consistency between two analyses of the same couplings**: V1's, with the declared
geometry, and the authors', with theirs. It supports the implementation and the declared-geometry
model together. It is not a precision test, and not independent data.

**Why 5 %.** The declared geometry is not the authors'. The fitted S scales as r³, so a 1 %
difference in the H–H distance is about 3 % in S. Benzene structures of different kinds
(equilibrium, vibrationally averaged, or derived from LC-NMR) differ by up to about 1 %. The
authors' SHAPE fits also weight the couplings in ways not yet known, and the rigid ratios are
about 1 % off. 5 % covers both for benzene. It gives **no quantitative bound** for
ortho-dichlorobenzene's ring distortions.

**It was chosen after exposure.** V0.1's scale check on one benzene row, recorded in the local
target record, implied an ortho distance within 1 % of the declared one.

**Reproduction** compares against the authors' own analysis. A reproduction claim requires
**documented geometry, objective, weights and corrections** (vibrational or other), all four. It
stays **pending** until they are obtained. Then:
- for a table, a cell agrees when |S − S_pub| ≤ max(printed σ, half a unit in the last printed
  digit);
- for a figure, when |S − S_pub| ≤ 2p.

If the geometry is obtained but the weights or the objective are not, the fallback is fixed:
- a weighted least-squares fit of all the condition's printed couplings, with weights 1/σ²;
- the authors' geometry;
- no vibrational or other correction, unless the source documents one, in which case it is
  applied as documented.

Every result of the fallback, pass or fail, is labelled **"approximate reproduction (assumed
weights 1/σ²)"**, and is not a reproduction claim.

## 7. Cells, labels and aggregation

- **The roster is frozen:** 15 cells for benzene, 44 for ortho-dichlorobenzene. The label
  function refuses a set of cells that misses or repeats one.
- **Each cell** is *agrees*, *disagrees* or *pending*. It is pending, in this order of reasons,
  for:
  - `rank-unverified`: the fit's rank is not verified, so no named component is estimated
    (V1.0 D-8);
  - `no-target`: there is no target, or the target is pending;
  - `s_zz-target-pending`: S_xx − S_yy whose condition has no published S_zz.
- **The dataset label is the first of these that applies:**
  1. **unresolved failure:** any disagreement without a reviewed README entry.
  2. **explained discrepancy:** every disagreement has a reviewed README entry (estimand,
     geometry, vibrational corrections, solvent). It is **not counted as validation**.
  3. **pending:** no cell is compared.
  4. **partially supported:** every compared cell agrees, and some are pending.
  5. **experimentally supported:** every cell of the roster is compared and agrees.
- **Each label is reported with its counts:** roster, compared, agreeing, disagreeing, pending.
- **Every label carries** "development data, no independent holdout", and for
  ortho-dichlorobenzene the spot-check note while that is open.
- **Sensitivity (ortho-dichlorobenzene).** The full-data label is primary. The label is also
  recomputed without II/300 K and without II/302 K, each with its counts. If either differs from
  the full-data label, the result is reported as *fragile*.
- **Not part of any label:** adequacy, the ratio residuals and the scaled standard errors. They
  are reported beside it.

## 8. Amendments

- **The original is kept.** This protocol, as merged with tag `v0.3-preregistration`, stays in the
  history, and **every result made under it is kept and reported**, even if a later amendment
  changes the analysis.
- **The protocol revision** is `prereg.protocol_sha256`: the SHA-256 of the canonical JSON
  (sorted keys, no whitespace) **without the amendment ledger**. So the ledger can name it
  without hashing itself. Every report and every retained result records the revision it used.
- **Every amendment is recorded** as an entry in the JSON's `amendments` and in this document.
  The schema enforces its fields:
  - its date, kind and reason, and whether it is post hoc;
  - the protocol revision before and after it;
  - for a data or target correction, also the source evidence, the artifact's path, and the
    artifact's SHA-256 before and after.
- **post hoc.** An amendment is *post hoc* if it was made after **any** V1 experimental result
  (of either dataset) had been seen, whether or not the affected fit was rerun. A post hoc result
  is labelled so wherever it appears, beside the original one.
- **Corrections:**
  - **A data correction** fixes a transcription error, found by the owner's spot-check say. It
    must be verifiable against the source, with the source page or cell as its evidence, and the
    before/after hashes of the data file.
  - **A target correction** (a re-digitization, say) follows the same rule.
  - Both are allowed at any time. Each follows the post hoc rule above.

## 9. Reproducing the targets

Both commands are run from the repository root, with the two PDFs from `v0-sources.json` in
`datasets-local/sources/`. The tools' dependencies are the locked `datasets` group, and
`--locked` makes the replay fail if the lock would change. The tools verify the PDFs' and the
transcription's checksums.

```sh
uv run --locked --group datasets python tools/datasets/digitize_figure.py \
    --pdf datasets-local/sources/chandrakumar-burnell-1993-bmr-issue.pdf \
    --couplings datasets-local/odcb-chandrakumar1993/couplings.csv \
    --out datasets-local/odcb-chandrakumar1993/targets.csv
uv run --locked --group datasets python tools/datasets/taggar2006_targets.py \
    --pdf datasets-local/sources/taggar-2006.pdf --out datasets-local/benzene-taggar2006-targets/targets.csv
```

Each command prints the SHA-256 recorded in the JSON.

`tests/test_digitize_figure.py` runs the digitizer on synthetic skewed scans. It checks:
- marker reading;
- the stacked and side-by-side splits;
- the refusal of a missing tick;
- end to end: correct assignment, wrongly keyed conditions and a missing marker (pending, not
  targets), and the Fig. 2 agree, disagree and unavailable outcomes;
- the refusal of an unrecorded transcription.

## 11. The restricted-value scan and its limits

`tools/datasets/restricted_scan.py` scans **every blob in git's index**, what would be committed.

**How it reads a blob.**
- It decodes each blob as UTF-8, or as UTF-16/32 by byte-order mark or by its NUL pattern.
- It parses every numeric literal: a sign (including U+2212), a decimal point or a colon, a
  leading point, an exponent. It compares the literals **by value**, so trailing zeros,
  scientific notation and the colon form all match.

**The two tiers.**
- **Everywhere:** the printed o-dichlorobenzene couplings, and every target as written and at
  four decimals, matched by literals of four or more significant digits.
- **In `docs/phase2/` and `docs/validation/`:** the targets at three decimals as well.

**The exceptions.**
- Six single lines, by hash, each verified to exist in the last commit before the first exposure
  (2026-11-18): coincidences in a Phase 1 mockup and in two browser tests.
- The `upload-time` fields of `uv.lock`.

**A hit is reported** as `path:line`, without the value.

**What it does not find:**
- values rounded to fewer digits than their tier;
- values spelled in words, split across lines, or inside images, PDFs or compressed files;
- values recoverable by arithmetic from other numbers (§6 keeps those local);
- anything outside git's index.

The scan runs only where the local files exist, so CI skips it. It is part of each step's local
check before a commit.
